# Hiring Flow User Guide

## When You Need a New Agent

If you request a task type we do not support yet (like image generation), you will see a hiring request. The job status becomes `AWAITING_HIRE` until approval.

## What Happens When You Approve

1. A basic agent is created or activated for the required role
2. Your job waits (status: `AWAITING_HIRE`) until hiring completes
3. Planning resumes automatically and the job moves to `PLAN_PROPOSED`

## What Happens When You Reject

- The hiring request is rejected
- The job is cancelled

## Development Times (Estimated)

- Image Generator: 2-3 hours
- Data Analyst: 3-4 hours
- Code Writer: 2.5-3 hours
- Video Editor: 4-5 hours
- Audio Producer: 3 hours
- Translator: 1.5 hours
- Researcher: 2 hours

Times are estimates and may vary.
